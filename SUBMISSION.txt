Description
SentinelForge links an interactive evidence workbench to a real bilingual
Tiny Aya Global refusal-intervention pilot. CuratorKIT cleans and deduplicates
calibration examples, SafeTune calibrates an inference-time refusal direction,
and AuditKIT invokes both baseline and intervention model paths on held-out
English and Bengali requests. The app exposes recorded answers, per-answer
review, evidence import and JSON export.

Findings
Across six harmful requests, explicit refusal rose from 5/6 to 6/6. Across six
benign requests, false refusal remained 0/6. Mean generation latency increased
from 5.143 to 7.107 seconds. Ratings are AI-assisted and await independent human
review. The baseline non-refusal was already a safe anti-fraud warning, so no
reduction in harmful completions is established.

Limitations and provenance
This is a 12-prompt pilot, not a general safety benchmark. Many answers are
truncated at 128 generated tokens. No trained checkpoint or adapter is claimed:
the intervention changes activations at inference. Model revision and steering
tensor were not saved. The visual prototype predates the event; the real run,
evidence integration and review interface were prepared on 1 November 2026.
